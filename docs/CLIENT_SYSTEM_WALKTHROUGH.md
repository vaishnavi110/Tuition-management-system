# How Your New Tuition Management System Works
**A Simple, Step-by-Step Guide for Management & Teachers**  
*Prepared for: Academy Management*  
*Version: 1.0 (Non-Technical Overview)*  

---

## 1. Why We Are Building This
Right now, managing **200+ students on WhatsApp** is overwhelming:
* Sending Google Meet links individually every day takes hours.
* Calculating hours attended in notebooks or Excel sheets leads to errors and disputes.
* Tracking who paid fees via GPay and who hasn't is stressful.
* Calculating teacher salaries based on different grades takes days at the end of each month.

**The Solution:**  
A private, easy-to-use website where **Admin, Teachers, and Students** each have their own simple login. The system automatically tracks hours, calculates monthly student fee bills, and computes teacher payouts with zero manual math.

---

## 2. A Day in the Life: How Everyone Will Use the App

### 👩‍🎓 Step 1: For the Student (or Parent)
* **Simple Login:** The student logs into the portal using their mobile number/email and password.
* **One-Click Class Join & Auto-Attendance:** Right on their home screen, they see:
  * Teacher’s Name & Subject.
  * Today’s Class Timing.
  * A big green button: **"Join Google Meet"** (The moment they click it, the system automatically records their attendance and entry time!).
* **View Monthly Fee Bill & Instant Online Payment:**
  * Shows total hours attended this month.
  * Shows total fee amount calculated automatically based on their grade.
  * **"Pay Now" (Razorpay):** Parents can pay instantly using **GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, Cards, or NetBanking**.
  * Once paid, the bill immediately changes to **"PAID"** and generates an instant digital payment receipt.
  * *(Offline backup: Also shows your academy UPI QR code for direct bank transfers if preferred).*
* **Homework:**
  * See homework photos posted by the teacher.
  * Take a photo of their completed notebook and upload it in one click.

---

### 👨‍🏫 Step 2: For the Teacher
* **Schedule 1-on-1 Classes:**
  * The teacher logs in and sees only the students assigned to them.
  * They can set the class time and paste their **Google Meet link**.
  * If a student requests a timing change, the teacher can reschedule it directly in the app.
* **Submit Post-Class Log (Takes 30 seconds):**
  * Immediately after class, the teacher fills a quick form:
    1. **Date & Timing:** e.g., 5:00 PM to 6:30 PM (System automatically records **1.5 hours**).
    2. **Student Attendance:** Already pre-filled as **"Present (Auto-detected)"**! Teacher simply confirms or adjusts if needed.
    3. **Topics Covered:** e.g., *"Chapter 4: Quadratic Equations - Exercise 4.2 completed."*
    4. **Homework:** Upload a photo of the homework questions.
* **Live Earnings Tracker:**
  * The teacher can see their total hours taught this month and their running earnings.

---

### 👑 Step 3: For the Admin (You)
* **Master Control & Oversight:**
  * Add new students and teachers with one click.
  * Assign which teacher teaches which student.
  * Set the **Hourly Rate Card** for each grade (e.g., Grade 8 = ₹350/hr, Grade 10 = ₹450/hr, Grade 12 = ₹600/hr).
* **Automated Monthly Fee Invoices & Zero Manual Payment Checking:**
  * On the 1st of every month, the system generates a neat fee bill for all 200 students automatically:  
    $$\text{Total Hours Attended} \times \text{Student Grade Rate} = \text{Total Fee}$$
  * When parents pay online via Razorpay, the system **marks the bill as PAID instantly** without you having to check your bank SMS or GPay app!
  * For offline cash or direct bank transfers, you can still manually click **"Mark as Paid"**.
* **Automated Teacher Salary Reports:**
  * The system calculates each teacher’s salary based on the hours they taught for each grade.
  * You know the exact payout for every teacher with zero manual calculation.
* **Zero Charge for Cancelled Classes:**
  * If a class was cancelled or marked "Absent," the system charges **₹0** for that class.

## 3. How Your Confirmed Requirements Are Handled

| What You Asked For | How the System Does It |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Strictly 1-on-1 Classes** | Every session is designed for 1 teacher and 1 student. No confusing group setups. |
| **Google Meet Links** | Teachers paste their own Google Meet link when scheduling. Students just click one button to join. |
| **Teachers Can Reschedule** | Teachers have direct permission to adjust timings without bothering you every time. |
| **Auto-Attendance** | Clicking "Join Google Meet" immediately timestamps and marks the student as Present. |
| **Online Payments (Razorpay)** | Parents pay via GPay/PhonePe/Card with one click. Invoices update to PAID automatically. |
| **Hourly Rates by Grade** | You can set different hourly charges for Grade 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, etc. |
| **GPay Payments** | Parents pay directly to your GPay. You enter the transaction ID in the app to mark it paid. |
| **No Charge for Cancelled Classes** | Only conducted, attended hours are added to the monthly bill. Cancelled classes are free. |
| **Homework via Photos** | Teachers and students can upload pictures of questions and notebook solutions. |
